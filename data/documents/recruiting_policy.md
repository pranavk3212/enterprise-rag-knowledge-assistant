# AcmeWorks Recruiting and Referrals Policy

Employees may refer candidates through the careers portal. A referral must be submitted before the candidate applies or within 48 hours after the application to be eligible for a referral bonus.

The referral bonus is $1,000 for an eligible full-time hire and is paid after the new employee completes 90 days of employment. Hiring managers are not eligible for a referral bonus for roles in their reporting chain.

Interview feedback must be submitted in the applicant tracking system within 24 hours of an interview. Interviewers must not request protected personal information from candidates.
