# AcmeWorks Procurement and Vendor Policy

Purchases up to $2,500 require manager approval. Purchases from $2,501 to $10,000 require department-head approval. Purchases above $10,000 require Finance approval and a signed vendor agreement.

Employees must use approved vendors when one is available. New vendors must complete a security and privacy review before receiving access to AcmeWorks data or systems.

No employee may sign a vendor contract unless they have written delegation from an authorized company signer. Forward all vendor contracts to Legal before agreeing to terms.
