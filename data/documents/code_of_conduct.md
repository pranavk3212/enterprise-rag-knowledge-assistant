# AcmeWorks Code of Conduct

AcmeWorks expects every employee, contractor, and intern to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, threats, and bullying are prohibited in person, in writing, and in online workspaces.

Employees may report a conduct concern to their manager, People Operations, or ethics@acmeworks.example. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern or participates in an investigation is prohibited.

Gifts from vendors or customers worth more than $50 must be disclosed to the employee's manager and People Operations. Cash and cash equivalents may never be accepted.
