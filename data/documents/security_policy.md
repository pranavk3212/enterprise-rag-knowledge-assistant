# AcmeWorks Information Security Policy

All AcmeWorks employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) for every corporate system. Passwords must be at least 14 characters, use a unique passphrase, and must not be reused across company accounts. Employees may use the approved password manager to create and store credentials.

Report a suspected phishing email, malware alert, lost device, or other security incident to security@acmeworks.example within one business hour. Do not forward suspicious messages to colleagues or click links to investigate them. The Security team will acknowledge reports through the service desk.

Company laptops inactive for 90 consecutive days must be returned to IT. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately through the IT service desk so that remote access can be revoked.
