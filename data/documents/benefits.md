# AcmeWorks Benefits Guide

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) each calendar year. Unused PTO can carry over up to five days into the following calendar year. Managers should approve or decline PTO requests within five business days.

AcmeWorks matches employee retirement contributions dollar for dollar up to 4% of eligible salary. New hires become eligible on their first day of employment. Employees can update their contribution election through the benefits portal.

The company provides medical, dental, and vision coverage. New employees have 30 days after their start date to enroll or decline coverage.
