# AcmeWorks New Hire Onboarding Guide

New hires must complete identity verification, security awareness training, and benefits enrollment tasks during their first 30 days. Managers must schedule a role orientation and introduce the new hire to their assigned onboarding buddy during the first week.

IT ships a standard laptop and charger before the employee's start date. New hires should confirm the asset tag in the IT portal within two business days of receiving the device.

New employees have a 90-day introductory period. During this period, managers should hold weekly check-ins to discuss expectations, training, and support.
