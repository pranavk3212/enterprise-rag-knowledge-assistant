# AcmeWorks Business Expense Policy

Employees may submit reasonable, business-related expenses through the expense portal. Submit itemized receipts within 30 days of purchase. Expenses submitted after 60 days require director approval.

For domestic business travel, the hotel guideline is up to $220 per night before taxes. Air travel longer than six hours may be booked in premium economy when approved by a director. Economy class is required for all other flights.

The meal allowance is up to $60 per day while traveling overnight for business. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved customer event. Personal travel extensions and minibar charges are never reimbursable.
