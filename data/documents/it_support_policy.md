# AcmeWorks IT Support and Device Policy

The IT service desk is available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time. Employees should submit normal support requests through the IT portal. For critical production-impacting issues, use the portal's "Urgent" priority and call the on-call number shown there.

Only company-managed devices may access internal systems containing confidential information. Employees may install approved software through the self-service catalog. Requests for other software require manager and IT Security approval.

When leaving AcmeWorks, employees must return all assigned equipment, including laptop, charger, security key, and peripherals, by their final working day.
