# AcmeWorks Data Privacy and Records Policy

Customer and employee personal data may be accessed only when needed for an authorized business purpose. Employees must not download personal data to an unapproved device or share it through personal email, consumer messaging apps, or public file-sharing services.

Business records must be retained according to the records schedule. Contracts and financial approvals must be stored in the approved document-management system for seven years. Teams must place a legal hold on relevant records when instructed by Legal.

Privacy requests or suspected data disclosures must be sent to privacy@acmeworks.example within one business day.
