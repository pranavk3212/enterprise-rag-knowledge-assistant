# AcmeWorks Business Continuity Policy

During a major operational disruption, the Incident Commander coordinates response activities and communicates status updates. Employees should follow official updates from the incident channel and avoid publishing unverified information externally.

Critical business services have recovery-time objectives of four hours. Teams that own critical services must test their recovery procedures at least once each year and record the results in the continuity register.

If the primary office is unavailable, employees should work remotely unless their manager communicates an alternative arrangement.
