# AcmeWorks Performance and Feedback Policy

Employees and managers hold quarterly growth conversations focused on goals, strengths, development, and support. Formal performance reviews occur twice each year, in June and December.

Employees who receive a performance improvement plan (PIP) are given clear objectives, support resources, and a review period of at least 45 calendar days. A PIP is intended to improve performance and does not automatically result in termination.

Employees may provide upward feedback through the anonymous engagement survey, which is issued twice a year.
