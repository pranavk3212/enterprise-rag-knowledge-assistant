# AcmeWorks Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Each team must publish its agreed in-office collaboration days in the department calendar. Employees must work from their approved home-work location unless their manager approves an exception.

Remote employees may claim up to $500 per calendar year for ergonomic equipment, including an adjustable chair, monitor, keyboard, mouse, or desk accessories. Submit itemized receipts through the expense portal within 30 days of purchase. Internet and personal utility bills are not reimbursable under this allowance.

Employees must use a private, secure workspace for confidential meetings. Public Wi-Fi may be used only through the company VPN.
