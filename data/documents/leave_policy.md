# AcmeWorks Leave and Attendance Policy

Employees who are sick should notify their manager as early as possible on the first day of absence. Employees may use accrued PTO for short illness-related absences without providing medical details.

For three or more consecutive workdays of illness, employees should contact People Operations to discuss leave options and any required documentation. Employees should not work while on approved medical leave unless People Operations provides written approval.

Employees are eligible for five paid bereavement days following the death of an immediate family member. Additional unpaid leave may be requested through People Operations.
